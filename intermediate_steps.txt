## Quick Sort

Pivot rule used by this project:

Initial pivot:

P8(10)

The exact partition sequence is printed by the program because Quick Sort
intermediate arrangements depend on the partition operation.

Important observation:

Quick Sort can change the relative positions of equal-weight packages.
Therefore, the final output is not guaranteed to preserve:

10: P4 -> P8
15: P2 -> P5
20: P1 -> P3 -> P6
